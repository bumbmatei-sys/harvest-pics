### Living in Freedom and Destiny

*   **Purpose of Freedom:** Deliverance, healing, and breaking strongholds are not ends in themselves; they are "cleaning the house" so you can walk in your destiny and calling.
*   **God’s Dream:** God is love and relationship. He has a big dream for the world and for you, so you must dream big.
*   **Biblical Mandate:**
    *   Created in God's image (Genesis 1) to be fruitful, multiply, and reign.
    *   **Reigning defined:** Bringing order to chaos (e.g., commanding peace over animals/circumstances).
    *   **Intimacy:** Genesis 2 depicts God kneeling to form man from dust—He is close, not distant.

### Core Identity Principles

*   **Leadership:** Everyone is a leader because everyone is called to have influence. You are the head, not the tail.
*   **Stewardship:** We own nothing; we are entrusted with gifts, talents, and resources to manage for God.
*   **Authority:** All authority comes from God. Rebellion is not an option (examples: Daniel, Joseph, Jesus).
*   **Honor:** A "culture of honor" means seeing the gold in others. You cannot fully receive from God or others without honor.
*   **Servanthood:** To be the greatest, you must become the servant of all.
*   **Power of the Tongue:** As co-creators made in God's image, your words have power. Confessing Scripture out loud is essential.

### Calling and Uniqueness

*   **Your "Garden":** God assigns you specific areas of influence (family, business, church). Faithfulness in the garden leads to more responsibility.
*   **Right to Exist:** You are not a mistake. You are God's masterpiece (*poema*), known before the foundation of the world.
*   **No Comparison:**
    *   Do not compare yourself to others; you have a unique capacity and anointing.
    *   **"Don't die a copy"**: Discover the specific warehouse of goods God prepared for you.
*   **Action:** You learn by doing. Insights come through practice, not just study.

### The Great Reset: Redemption

*   **Free Will:** Love requires choice. Both humans and angels were tested.
    *   *Angelic Test:* To serve humanity (Lucifer rebelled against this).
    *   *Human Test:* To trust God vs. the lie.
*   **The Fall:** Sin brought separation, shame, and decay.
*   **Total Restoration:** Jesus didn't just come to get souls to heaven; He came to restore *everything* (Acts 3:21).
*   **The Cross Accomplished:**
    *   Forgiveness of sins.
    *   Healing and wholeness.
    *   Deliverance (breaking of curses).
    *   Removal of accusation (legal rights of the enemy nailed to the cross).
    *   New creation/Born again status.

### Activation and The Father’s Heart

*   **Certainty of Salvation:** Doubt hinders freedom. Salvation is simple: Believe in the heart and confess with the mouth (Romans 10:9).
    *   *Why speak out loud?* You hear it, others hear it, and the spiritual world (demons) hears where you stand.
*   **Security in Love:** True security comes from knowing God loves you deeply.
*   **Jesus as Revelation:** Jesus is the "selfie of God"—if you see Him, you see the Father.
*   **The Prodigal Son:** The Father waits, runs to the lost, and restores full authority (ring, robe, sandals) immediately upon return.
*   **Healing Father Wounds:**
    *   Earthly fathers (absent, authoritarian, passive, abusive) distort our view of God.
    *   These wounds create a "hole in the soul."
    *   Healing comes through receiving the liquid love of the Father (symbolized by a fatherly hug) to restore identity.
